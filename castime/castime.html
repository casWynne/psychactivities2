CasTime
Round-robin activity scheduler
1
Set up your session — Choose how many minutes each activity slot lasts, and configure warnings, breaks, and sounds to suit your event.
2
Add activities — These are the stations or tasks that groups will rotate through. Drag to reorder them.
3
Add groups — Each group gets a label (A, B, C…) or a custom name. CasTime works out who goes where automatically.
4
Start the session — The timer counts down each slot. A "Change places!" overlay appears at the end of each round showing everyone their next activity.
5
During the session — Use ⏸ Pause to hold the clock, "Move on early" to skip a round, or tap ⚙ to toggle sounds and timing behaviour on the fly.
💡 The number of rounds equals the number of activities — so every group visits every station exactly once.
