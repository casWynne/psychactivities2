Build your spiky profile

Rate yourself on six dimensions of how minds work — each one a construct that shows up across the neurodiversity research. Nobody scores flat across the board; the jagged shape you get is the point. That spikiness is human, and for neurodivergent people it's often more pronounced.

Drag the sliders and watch your shape change.

This is a self-reflection tool for illustrating the idea of a "spiky profile" — not a psychometric test, diagnostic instrument, or clinical assessment. The six dimensions are simplified illustrations of cognitive constructs, and a 1–10 slider shows the shape of how someone experiences their own thinking, not a measured ability.
