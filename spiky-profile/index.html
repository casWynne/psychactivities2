Build your spiky profile

Rate yourself on six everyday skills. Nobody scores flat across the board — the jagged shape you get is the point. That spikiness is human, and for neurodivergent people it's just more pronounced.

Drag the sliders and watch your shape change.

Talking point: compare shapes with the person next to you. Two people can reach the same overall "score" through completely different spikes — which is exactly why a single number never captures how someone actually works.
